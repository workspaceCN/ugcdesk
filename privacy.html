UGC Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

UGC Desk is a free rate calculator and invoice maker for UGC creators, run by Mohammad Mubashiruddin.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we save your Google name, your email address, the times you sign in, and which tools you use in the app (for example "made a quote" or "downloaded a PDF"). We do not access anything else in your Google account, such as your contacts, Drive, Gmail or calendar.

What stays on your device

Your invoice details, client names, bank or UPI details and rates are stored only in your own browser. They are not sent to us.

Why we collect it

To know how many people use the app, to improve it, and to block misuse.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. It is kept in a private Google Sheet that only the owner can open.

Deleting your data

To have your name and email removed, email er.md.mubashir@gmail.com and we will delete it.
